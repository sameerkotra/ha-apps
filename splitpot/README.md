# Splitpot

> ⚠️ **Unofficial app.** This is not an official Home Assistant app and isn't affiliated with or
> endorsed by Home Assistant or Nabu Casa. It was built with Claude, Anthropic's AI model, and is
> provided as-is: try it out first, and keep your own backups.

Splitpot is a shared-expense splitter for your household that runs inside
Home Assistant. Everyone opens it from the Home Assistant sidebar and is
signed in as themselves, so there are no extra accounts or passwords. Make
groups, log who paid for what, and Splitpot works out who owes whom and the
fewest payments to settle up. Everything stays on your Home Assistant: no
cloud service, no published port.

## Features

- Groups for a household, a trip or a project, with one group as the start page
- Expenses split equally, by exact amounts or by percentages, always to the cent
- Per-group balances with "who pays whom" suggestions, and recorded settle-up payments
- Dashboard: overall balance, weekly and monthly spending, and an activity log
- Optional Home Assistant sensors with each person's balance
- People come from Home Assistant's own Persons; an Admin area for app
  settings, users and database backup/restore
