Tips for a clean scan
  • Lay the receipt flat on a dark surface with good light.
  • Keep the camera parallel to the paper and hold steady.
  • Make sure every price on the right edge is visible.
  • Got the receipt by email? Upload the PDF, each page is read like a photo.
