# Family Tree

> ⚠️ **Unofficial app.** This is not an official Home Assistant app and isn't affiliated with or
> endorsed by Home Assistant or Nabu Casa. It was built with Claude, Anthropic's AI model, and is
> provided as-is: try it out first, and keep your own backups.

A shared family tree for your household, right inside Home Assistant. Everyone
with a Home Assistant login can add people, families, photos and stories, and
every change is recorded with who made it and can be undone. The tree shows how
everyone is related to you, lists upcoming birthdays and anniversaries, and can
send an opt-in daily reminder to your phone. Photos live on Home Assistant's
`/share` folder (or a NAS mounted there), so backups stay small; an admin
chooses which optional parts of the app are switched on.

- People, families (adopted, step and foster too) and life events, with
  approximate dates or a day and month without a year
- An interactive tree around one person or the whole family, and a phone view
- "This is me": relationship names ("your first cousin once removed") and
  relationship search
- Photos and documents with face tagging, stories, sources and custom fields
- Upcoming birthdays and anniversaries, milestones, and phone reminders
- A family website export (a zip that opens offline), a wall chart and a book
- Full history with undo, a trash, and backup and restore for admins
- Optional: a places map (uses the internet), Indian relationship names and
  names in Telugu/Hindi script, tithi dates, and Indian ceremonies

See the Documentation tab (`DOCS.md`) for setup and the full guide.
