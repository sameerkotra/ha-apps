Transfers

Money moving between two of your own accounts — a payment to a credit card, or a transfer between checking/savings accounts — isn't real spending or income (SPEC.md section 8). Matches are by amount and date proximity, not merchant text — the two sides of a transfer almost never describe it the same way. An unambiguous match links automatically on import; anything with more than one possible match needs a human to pick below, and any single transaction can be force-checked from its own "Find match" button on the Transactions page.
