Categories & rules

A new transaction is categorized automatically on import: your rules below are tried first (most specific match wins — exact beats a prefix match beats a substring match; ties go to the longer pattern, then whichever rule was saved most recently), then the built-in rules further down, then a one-off AI guess if nothing matches, and finally "Uncategorized" if nothing works at all (SPEC.md section 9).
