Each transaction gets one category — from your rules, then the built-in ones, then the AI.

How this works

A new transaction is categorized automatically on import: your rules are tried first (most specific match wins — exact beats starts with beats contains; ties go to the longer text, then whichever rule was saved most recently), then the built-in rules for its direction, then a one-off AI guess if nothing matches, and finally “Uncategorized”. You can always change a transaction's category by hand.

Every category is Spending or Income. Whether a transaction counts as income or spending is still its direction: money in on a checking or savings account is income, whatever its category. The kind decides which list a category is offered from (money in on checking or savings shows Income first) and how Home breaks your income down. The AI is offered only the list for the transaction's direction. Money back on a card stays a card payment unless a rule says Refunds, and then it counts as spending going down.

Hidden categories stay on the transactions that have them and their rules still work; they just aren't offered in the menus. A colour is used for the category on Home and Compare.
